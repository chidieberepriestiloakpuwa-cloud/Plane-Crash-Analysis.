### PLANE-CRASH-ANALYSIS-

##### About This Analysis Report:
This project examined global plane crash data to uncover patterns, highlight risk factors, and provide actionable insights for aviation safety improvements.

#### DATA SUMMARY:
The dataset contained 28,457 crashes, with detailed statistics on passengers, crew, survival rates, regions, flight phases, aircraft types, and organizations involved.

#### OBJECTIVES:
1. Identified the most critical phases and conditions that led to crashes.
2. Compared crash distribution across continents, countries, and organizations.
3. Evaluated survival outcomes and influencing factors.
4. Recommended strategies to reduce risks and enhance safety.

#### KEY FINDINGS:
Landing and takeoff phases accounted for the majority of crashes.
Human factors such as fatigue and training gaps remained significant contributors.
Older aircraft models (e.g., Douglas C‑47 Skytrain) showed disproportionately high crash counts.
Survival rates stayed below 50%, with terrain proximity to airports influencing outcomes.

#### RECOMMENDATIONS:
Strengthened pilot training and fatigue management programs.
Implemented predictive maintenance and stricter inspection protocols.
Modernized fleets by retiring aging aircraft and incentivizing adoption of advanced safety systems.
